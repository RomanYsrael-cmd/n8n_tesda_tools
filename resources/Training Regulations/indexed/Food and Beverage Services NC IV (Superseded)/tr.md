SECTION 1 FOOD AND BEVERAGE SERVICE NC IV QUALIFICATION

The Food and Beverage Service NC IV Qualification consists of
competencies that a person must achieve to operate a restaurant and/or coffee
shop, managing a team of food service and/or food preparation staff for guests in
hotels, motels, restaurants, clubs, canteens, resorts, luxury liners and cruise ships
within the company’s acceptable standards. Specifically, it includes competencies on
operating a food outlet, preparing tenders for catering contracts, developing a food
safety program and planning for coffee shop operations. S/he is also able to
implement and/or execute plans and monitor expenses to generate the required
gross profit margins.

This Qualification is packaged from the competency map of the Tourism
Sector (Hotels and Restaurants) as shown in Annex A.
